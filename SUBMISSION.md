## Suggested description

Dark Relic is a solo dark-fantasy extraction game set in Widowfen. As the Warden, combine sword combos, a relic shockwave, and a temporary Fury transformation to defeat the Bellkeeper and recover Blackbell. Watch enemy attack warnings, manage stamina and healing, and survive the final ward countdown to bank your loot. Death loses the current haul; successful escapes fund a permanent health upgrade.

The demo features one compact Unreal level, three enemy roles, voiced combat reactions, directional knockback, an enraged boss phase, atmospheric sound, and an escalating extraction finale. It uses licensed/premade character assets and AI-assisted development, with a generated Blackbell prop. It does not claim that all art was created during the event.
