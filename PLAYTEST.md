# Final-day human playtest

Use the **Dark Relic Warden Candidate** shortcut after its package has passed automated validation. The Enhanced Candidate has the earlier combat set. Keep both earlier shortcuts available for rollback. Do not add smoke, capture or profiling command-line flags to a human play session.

- [ ] Move, turn and fight around Widowfen. Confirm the Warden stays readable against buildings and ground, with no path blocked by new props.
- [ ] Land light and heavy attacks. Confirm sparks and impact sounds coincide with contact. Press M to disable shake and confirm controls still feel responsive.
- [ ] Chain three LMB strikes, waiting for recovery between clicks. Confirm the third strike feels stronger and shows Sunder. Pause beyond the combo window or use a different action, then confirm the chain starts again at one.
- [ ] Press F near enemies in front and behind you. Confirm the shockwave appears on contact, cover blocks damage, and enemies beyond its range remain safe. Try F again during cooldown and with low stamina; confirm no extra cast or stamina drain.
- [ ] Press R during combat. Confirm Fury's active timer and stronger hits, then its expiry and cooldown. Q must still heal; R on the result screen must start a new run.
- [ ] Approach each enemy. Confirm its warning can be distinguished from ambience and footsteps.
- [ ] Fight the Bellkeeper. Step outside one marked area attack, dodge another, and confirm its below-half-health enrage is understandable. Report any damage that felt unavoidable or occurred outside the ring.
- [ ] Defeat the Bellkeeper and pick up Blackbell. Start extraction, step out to cancel it, then restart the full countdown. Confirm accelerating bells, readable ward effects and the escape celebration.
- [ ] Check banked credits, buy Resolve if affordable, and restart. Confirm the upgrade and bank persist after quitting and relaunching.
- [ ] Press Escape. Confirm the game closes normally.

Record: package/revision, completion or failure, roughly how long the run took, any stuck location, unfair hit, unreadable scene, audio clipping or severe frame stutter. A successful automated smoke run does not complete these human checks.
