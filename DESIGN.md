# Dark Relic visual system

Retain the existing Unreal canvas HUD, medium engine font, health and stamina bars and stable control locations. Warm gold signals objectives and extraction; amber rings and explicit text mark area danger. Enrage has a named state as well as a warmer color. Effects must stay below enemy labels and leave the view readable.

Environment: neutral moon fill, softer shadows, warm route lanterns, subdued fog and restrained cyan. Reuse verified meshes at path edges without narrowing combat or extraction space. Camera impulse is short and optional.
