# Dark Relic product context

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Hackathon players on a Windows desktop use keyboard and mouse to fight through Widowfen, retrieve Blackbell and escape. The encounter must teach danger while preserving agency and fast control response.

## Personality
Ominous, weathered, deliberate. The player reads an armored silhouette against a dim swamp settlement, with warm lanterns marking the safe route.

## Principles
Warnings communicate timing and extent before damage. Feedback distinguishes a landed hit from a swing. The finale celebrates earned escape without blocking restart. Preserve the working package and existing controls.

## Accessibility
Use words, shapes and progress bars alongside color. Keep shake subtle and provide a motion toggle. Do not obscure targets with full-screen effects.
