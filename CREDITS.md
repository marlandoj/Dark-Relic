# Dark Relic asset credits and provenance

Prepared September 12, 2026. This record identifies sources; it does not grant rights to third-party content.

- **Gameplay and procedural sound:** project-authored C++ rules, Unreal integration, combat effects, synthesized impacts, ambience, footsteps, warnings, and extraction sounds.
- **Character models and animation:** Epic Games, Paragon Greystone and Paragon Minions. Recorded Warden voices and enemy pain variants come from the installed Greystone pack. Enemy variants reuse those recordings with pitch changes; they are not original recorded performances.
- **Environment and props:** Poly Haven CC0 assets, including work by Amal Kumar, Rob Tuytel, Rico Cilliers, James Ray Cock, Kless Gyzen, Serhii Khromov, and Ulan Cabanilla. Powered by Poly Haven.
- **Blackbell prop:** Tripo v3.1 image-to-model generation from the project's fal.ai concept image, followed by local material/collision preparation. Original generation receipt records September 11, 2026, seed 1847 and 50 credits. Concept images are not represented as gameplay screenshots.
- **Engine:** Unreal Engine by Epic Games. Retain the included engine and third-party notices.

## Source records

Both mark AI usage as disallowed. Their source assets were not supplied to generation models.

[Poly Haven's license](https://polyhaven.com/license) identifies its assets as CC0. The private project manifest retains exact source files, authors, and checksums.

[Tripo terms](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms), section 5.2, distinguish free and paid users' output rights. The private project retains generation and payment evidence; no billing details are included in this package.

The [Fab Standard License summary](https://www.fab.com/eula) describes distribution of incorporated assets within projects and excludes standalone asset redistribution. Public listings do not establish which terms this account accepted. The accepted acquisition/license records for the two Fab packs still need to be retained before declaring submission clearance complete.

The public source repository excludes third-party source meshes, textures, animations, and generated source models. The playable candidate contains cooked content used by the game.
